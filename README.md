# Vendor Performance Analytics

## 📊 Project Overview

An end-to-end Vendor Performance Analytics project designed to analyze vendor sales, purchasing, inventory, profitability, and operational performance.

The project uses Python, SQL, SQLite, and Power BI to transform raw business data into actionable insights for vendor management and inventory optimization.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Identify high-performing and underperforming vendors
- Analyze vendor contribution to total purchases and sales
- Evaluate vendor profitability and profit margins
- Identify slow-moving inventory
- Analyze inventory turnover
- Evaluate the relationship between purchase volume and unit purchase price
- Identify brands with low sales but high profit margins
- Estimate capital tied up in unsold inventory
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard for business decision-making

---
